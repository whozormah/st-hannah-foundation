# Editing the website — a guide for staff

For the Foundation's Content Manager, Administrator and Owner. It covers
everything about the public website that can be changed from the admin,
without a developer.

## Signing in

Choose **Staff Sign In** at the very bottom of any page (or go to **/admin**
on the website) and sign in with your email and password. If
you have forgotten your password, choose **Forgot password?**: a reset link
arrives by email and works once.

What you see depends on your role. Content Managers see the **Content**
section; Case Officers and Finance do not see it at all.

## Where things are

Everything that appears on the website is under **Content** in the menu.

| To change… | Open |
|---|---|
| The homepage: its sections, their order and their headings | **Pages → Homepage** |
| A programme's page | **Programmes** |
| An impact story | **Impact Stories** |
| The quotes from beneficiaries and volunteers | **Testimonials** |
| The team page | **Leadership** and **Volunteer Profiles** |
| The gallery | **Gallery**, **Featured Events**, **Video Highlights** |
| The volunteer page | **Volunteer Opportunities** and **Volunteer Benefits** |
| The featured campaign on the homepage | **Campaign Stories** |
| The Foundation's introduction, vision, mission and the founder's message | **About the Foundation** |
| The "Before you apply" notes | **Apply for Support Page** |
| Figures such as "500+ widows supported" | **Statistics** |
| Photographs and their descriptions | **Media Library** |
| Email, phone, office addresses, social links (Administrators and Owner) | **Site Settings** |

**In-kind Categories** and **Donate Page** are marked "Not shown on the
website at the moment". Changing them changes nothing that visitors see.

## Draft, preview, publish

Programmes, stories, testimonials, the homepage and the other lists work the
same way:

1. **Make your changes** and choose **Save Draft**. Nothing changes on the
   website. You can come back to a draft later.
2. **Preview**: the preview button at the top of the editor opens the page
   in a new tab, showing your unpublished changes. A dark bar at the bottom
   says *"Preview: you are seeing unpublished changes"*. Only signed-in
   editors see a preview; visitors see the published website. Choose **Exit
   preview** when you are done.
3. **Publish changes** puts them on the website straight away. The next
   person to open the page sees them. No developer and no waiting.

**Changed your mind?**

- **Revert to published** throws away a draft and goes back to what is on
  the website.
- **Unpublish** takes the record off the website without deleting it.
- **Versions** lists every saved version. Open one to compare it with the
  current one, and choose **Restore this version** to bring it back.

Pages such as **About the Foundation**, **Statistics** and **Site Settings**
have no drafts: **Save** publishes immediately, so check your wording first.

## Web addresses

A programme's or story's address ends in its **Web Address**, such as
`medical-aid` in `/programs/medical-aid`. Leave it empty and it is filled in
from the title; whatever you type is tidied into lowercase words joined by
hyphens.

You can change it at any time. Once the record is published, the old address
keeps working: visitors, and links shared on WhatsApp or in newsletters, are
sent to the new one. Administrators can see these under **Redirects**, and
add their own for addresses from the old website.

## Deleting

Something still in use cannot be deleted: a photograph on a page, for
instance. The message lists where it is used, such as *Page "Homepage"*.
Remove it from there first, then delete it. To take a record off the website
without deleting it, use **Unpublish**.

## The homepage

**Pages → Homepage** lists the homepage's sections from top to bottom.

- **Reorder** by dragging a section by its handle.
- **Remove** a section with its menu. It disappears from the homepage; the
  content it showed (the stories, the testimonials) is not deleted.
- **Add** a section with **Add Section**, then choose one of the fourteen:

| Section | What it shows | What you write |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | The large rotating banner | Each slide's heading, text, image and button |
| Vision and Mission | The introduction, vision and mission | Nothing: it shows **About the Foundation** |
| Programme Cards | The first four programmes | Small heading, heading |
| Gallery Strip | One tile per gallery area | Small heading, heading, introduction |
| Statistics Row | The homepage's four figures | Small heading, heading, introduction |
| Donation Call to Action | The featured campaign stories | Small heading, introduction |
| Story Cards | The first three impact stories | Small heading, heading, introduction |
| Testimonials | The published testimonials | Small heading, heading, introduction |
| Leadership Preview | The first three leaders | Small heading, heading, introduction |
| Call to Action | The closing invitation to give, volunteer or partner | Small heading, heading, introduction |
| Rich Text | Formatted text | Headings, bold, italic, lists, quotations, links |
| Image and Text | A photograph beside text | Heading, text, image, which side, a button |
| Quote | A quotation | The words and who said them |
| Video | A picture that opens a YouTube or Vimeo video | Heading, text, picture, the video's address |

A new section starts with the website's usual wording, ready to edit. To
break a Call to Action heading over two lines, start a new line where it
should break.

Links must go to a page on this website (starting with **/**, such as
`/donate`) or to a full address starting with **https://**, **mailto:** or
**tel:**. Anything else is refused when you publish. A video link must be
the video itself, such as `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…`, not the
YouTube home page.

## Events

For a fundraising event, such as the Widows Program, open **Events** and add
one: its title, date, venue, a few lines on why it matters, and a picture.
Optionally add a short silent video, the full video, and the flyer. Use
**Picture Caption** to say when the picture or video is from ("Widows Program
2025"), so last year's footage is never taken for this year's event.

To show it, open **Pages → Homepage**, choose **Add Section → Event Appeal**,
pick the event and drag the section to where you want it (straight after the
hero while the appeal runs). It changes by itself with the date:

- **Before the day:** a countdown, and buttons to give.
- **On the day:** "Happening today".
- **After the day:** your **Thank-You Message** and up to four photographs
  from **After the Event**, and a button to support the next programme.

When it has run its course, remove the section from the homepage.

Videos must be MP4 files of 50 MB or less; photographs 10 MB or less.

## About the Foundation

**About the Foundation** holds the introduction, vision and mission shown in
the homepage's Vision and Mission section, and the founder's message on the
About page.

## Campaign stories

A campaign story (such as Esther's) can have a **Video**. It plays silently
in place of the main picture, with a **Tap for sound** button that starts it
again from the beginning with sound. Portrait videos from a phone suit the
panel, and subtitles recorded in the video stay fully visible. Leave it empty
to show the picture.

## Photographs

Every photograph is in the **Media Library**, once, with a **description**:
a sentence saying what the picture shows, read aloud to people who cannot
see it. An image cannot be saved without one.

- Describe **only what is visible**. "A woman in a blue headscarf receives a
  bag of food" — not a name, a place or an event the picture does not show.
- The descriptions carried over from the old website were drafted by the
  developer. Check each one and tick **Description Approved by the
  Foundation** when it is right.
- To use a photograph, choose it from the library in the field that shows
  images. Changing a description updates it everywhere the photograph
  appears.

Uploading new photographs becomes available once the website's Cloudflare
storage is connected. Until then, choose from the images already in the
library.

## Statistics

Each figure is stored once, in **Statistics**, and every page that shows it
reads it from there, so two pages can never disagree. Under each figure it
says which pages show it.

Every figure has **Where This Figure Comes From** and **Verified On**. When
the Foundation has checked a figure, record where it comes from and the
date. Never publish a figure the Foundation cannot back up.

## What to write, and what never to write

- **Quotations** must be words someone actually said, with their permission.
  Never write a quotation for someone.
- **Figures** must come from the Foundation's own records.
- **People in photographs** should be pictured with their agreement,
  especially beneficiaries.

## When something does not work

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| *"This field is required"* | A heading, a description or another required field is empty. |
| *"Use a page on this site (starting with /) or a full address…"* | A link is not allowed. Fix the address. |
| *"Use the address of the video itself"* | The video link points to YouTube or Vimeo in general, not to a video. |
| *"Only the homepage (home) is built from sections"* | Other pages have fixed layouts; edit their content in their own section of the admin. |
| You cannot find **Content** in the menu | Your role does not include website content. Ask the Owner. |
| A change is not on the website | Check you chose **Publish changes**, not only **Save Draft**. |
| *"…is still used, so it cannot be deleted"* | Remove it from the records listed, then delete it. |
| *"Use lowercase letters and numbers joined by hyphens"* | The web address has characters it cannot use. |
