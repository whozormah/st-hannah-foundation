# Handling requests about personal information

Internal. Implements PRV-11 of the Product & Architecture Specification.

**Status: draft for legal review.** Items marked **TBC** depend on decision 3
(named data protection contact) and on counsel.

Anyone can ask what information we hold about them, or ask us to correct,
delete or stop using it. The public side of this is the "Your rights" section
of the Privacy Policy (`/privacy`).

## Steps

1. **Recognise the request.** It may arrive by email, phone, social media or
   in person, and it does not have to use legal language. Pass it to the data
   protection contact (**TBC**) the same day.
2. **Log it** with the date received and what is being asked.
3. **Confirm identity** before disclosing or changing anything — for example
   by replying to the email address that made the original submission, or
   asking for the reference number the Foundation sent (`SHA-`, `SHV-`,
   `SHF-`, `SHP-`, `SHC-`).
4. **Find the information.** Search every place it can be:
   - the Foundation's submission inboxes (search the person's name, email
     address and reference number)
   - the Paystack dashboard, for online donations
   - Resend's sending logs
5. **Act on the request.**
   - *Copy:* send the information in a readable form.
   - *Correct:* update it and confirm the change.
   - *Delete:* delete it from every place in step 4, unless the law requires
     us to keep it (for example, donation records) — and if so, explain why.
   - *Withdraw consent / object:* stop the processing and confirm.
6. **Reply** within the period the law requires. *Confirm the period with
   counsel.* Say what was done, and remind the person they can complain to
   the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## After Phase 3

Once submissions are stored in the database, step 4 becomes a search in the
staff portal, and deletion is replaced by anonymisation (PRV-07). This
procedure is updated at that point.
